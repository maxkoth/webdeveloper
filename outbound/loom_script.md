# 90-Second Loom Script + Shot List

**Goal:** prove you can ship, fast — not pitch. The agency owner should finish
thinking *"this person clearly builds real things, and I could put my logo on it."*

**Before you record**
- Have the actual app/build open and working in a browser tab or simulator.
- Have a second tab with the agency's site open (you'll name-drop them once).
- Close Slack/email notifications. Webcam bubble on (small, bottom corner) — a face builds trust.
- Talk like you're showing a peer, not presenting to a client. Energy up, no script-reading voice.
- Record one throwaway take first to warm up. Total target: **85–95 seconds.**

---

## Timeline

### 0:00–0:10 — Hook (face on cam, app on screen)
> "Hey [FIRST NAME] — saw [HOOK about their work], so I figured I'd just show you
> what I do instead of writing paragraphs. This is a [type of app] I built and
> handed off for an agency to rebrand. 60 seconds."

**Shot:** Your face, then cut to the live app's home screen.

---

### 0:10–0:35 — Show the core thing working (screen only, cursor moving)
> "Here's the one flow that matters — [the main user action]. Watch."

- Do the **single most impressive end-to-end action live**. Click through it. Don't narrate every button — let it work.
- If there's an AI feature: trigger it and let the real output appear on screen. Real > described.

**Shot:** Full screen, slow deliberate clicks. Zoom/highlight the cursor if your tool allows.

---

### 0:35–0:55 — Prove it's real, not a demo (screen)
> "This isn't a mockup — it's [hosted / in the app store / handling real data].
> Built with [TECH], shipped in [TIMEFRAME]."

- Show ONE piece of proof: the live URL bar, a deployed dashboard, an app-store listing, or real data loading.
- Keep tech name-drops to a single sentence. Owners care that it works, not your stack.

**Shot:** Address bar / deploy dashboard / store page — whatever proves "this is live."

---

### 0:55–1:15 — The white-label point (split: app + quick cam)
> "Here's the part for you: everything here is white-label. Your brand, your colors,
> your client relationship — I'm the build team behind the curtain. The client never
> knows I exist unless you want them to."

- If you have a before/after of the SAME build under two different brandings, flash it here. Strongest possible proof.

**Shot:** Quick toggle between two brand themes if you have it; otherwise back to your face.

---

### 1:15–1:30 — Soft, async CTA (face on cam)
> "If [AGENCY] ever has a client who wants an app or an AI feature and you'd rather
> not hire or turn it down — I'm the option in your back pocket. No pressure. Just
> reply and I'll send one more example that fits your niche. Thanks for the 90 seconds."

**Shot:** Face on cam, relaxed. Smile. End.

---

## Shot list (quick reference)

| Time      | On screen                          | You doing                          |
|-----------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| 0:00–0:10 | Face → app home                    | Hook, name their work              |
| 0:10–0:35 | App, core flow                     | Run the #1 action live             |
| 0:35–0:55 | Live URL / store / real data       | Prove it's shipped + name tech once|
| 0:55–1:15 | Brand toggle or face               | Explain white-label                |
| 1:15–1:30 | Face                               | Soft async CTA                     |

## Recording tips
- **Loom / Tella / Screen Studio** all work. Trim dead air at start/end after recording.
- Rename the Loom title to something personal: "For [AGENCY] — 90-sec build walkthrough".
- Loom shows you when they watch it — that's your signal to follow up.
- Re-record the 0:00–0:10 hook per agency; keep the middle the same. ~2 min of work per target.

## Do NOT
- Don't show a slide deck or a "services" page.
- Don't read this script word-for-word — bullet it and riff.
- Don't list every feature. One flow, shown well, beats ten described.
