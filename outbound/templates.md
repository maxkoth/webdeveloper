# Cold Outbound Templates — White-Label App / AI Dev for Agencies

**How to use these**
- Fill the `[BLANKS]` per target before sending. Never send with a blank still in it.
- `[AGENCY]` = the agency's name. `[HOOK]` = one specific, true thing about *their*
  work (a project on their site, a service they push, a recent post). The hook is
  the whole game — it proves you actually looked.
- Lead with their work, not yours. You're a peer offering capacity, not a vendor pitching.
- Keep it short. If it scrolls on a phone, cut it.
- Pick ONE variant per person. Don't send the same person two.

**Filling `[HOOK]` well (do this, not the lazy version)**
- ✅ "saw the [client] rebuild you shipped — the booking flow is clean"
- ✅ "you lead with Webflow + lifecycle marketing, not generic 'digital'"
- ❌ "love your website" / "great work you guys do" (reads as a mail-merge)

---

## Variant 1 — The capacity offer (email, ~70 words)

**Subject:** quick one re: [AGENCY] + dev capacity

Hi [FIRST NAME],

[HOOK] — clearly you've got the client side dialed.

I'm a developer who white-labels app and AI builds for agencies like [AGENCY]:
you keep the relationship and the brand, I'm the build team behind the curtain.

If you ever turn down (or sub out) dev-heavy work, might be worth a chat.
Worth a 15-min call, or should I send a couple examples first?

— [YOUR NAME]

### Notes on deliverability & compliance
- Personalize the `[HOOK]` for *every* send — it's also what keeps you out of spam folders.
- B2B cold email is legal in many places but regulated (CAN-SPAM in the US, GDPR/PECR
  in the EU/UK, CASL in Canada). Include a real reply path and honor opt-outs.
- Sending volume: warm up a new domain slowly. High volume from a cold domain gets flagged.
