### The idea
Collectors and resellers deal with a slow, manual job: identifying a sports card,
pulling its details, and figuring out what it's worth — one card at a time. I set
out to make that instant: **point a phone at a card, and the app recognizes it with
AI and surfaces the info that matters.**

### What I built
A consumer-facing **app + website** with one core experience that had to be great:
**scan a card → AI identifies it → details come back in seconds.** Everything else
(navigation, lists, the marketing site) supports that single moment. I built the
full thing: product design, the AI scanning flow, the app, and the website.
